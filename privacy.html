StayProof Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

StayProof counts the days you spend in each country so you can stay within visa, tax and health-insurance limits. It is built so that your travel history never leaves your iPhone. We don't collect any data.

What stays on your device

  • Trips you enter — places, dates and notes.
  • Automatic tracking (optional) — if you turn it on, iOS's low-power significant-change and visit monitoring tells the app roughly where you are, and the app records which country (or US state / Canadian province) you were in each day. Coordinates are not stored; only the place and the day.
  • Photo rebuild (optional) — the app reads the date and location of your photos and matches them to countries with a border map built into the app. Photos and their locations are never uploaded or copied.
  • Settings — your passport country, the rules you track, an optional name for your PDF report.

This data is stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone (and in the widget, which reads it from the same device). Backups exist only if you export one yourself.

What we do NOT do

  • No account, sign-in, server or cloud database.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. No third-party SDKs at all.
  • We never sell or share your data — we never receive it.

Apple services the app uses

  • Location name lookup. With automatic tracking on, the app asks Apple's geocoding service (the same one Maps uses) to name the place for a location sample, which sends that coordinate to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. When offline, the built-in border map is used instead. Photo rebuild always uses the built-in map.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Notifications are local — scheduled on your iPhone, with no push server.

Deleting your data

Delete trips in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

StayProof is not directed to children under 13.

Not advice

StayProof's results are planning estimates only — not legal, immigration or tax advice. Official border records (such as the EU Entry/Exit System) and tax authorities are authoritative.
