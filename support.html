How are days counted?

Any part of a day in a place counts as a day there, and travel days count for every place you were in that day. Schengen uses a rolling 180-day window; the US Substantial Presence Test uses this year plus ⅓ of last year plus ⅙ of the year before; provincial health rules use a rolling 12 months; tax-residency rules use your tax year.

My count differs from the official EU check

Official border records (EES) are authoritative. Check that every trip in the last 180 days is in StayProof with the right entry and exit days, and that long-stay visa or residence-permit periods are marked "not counted".

Automatic tracking

Turn it on in Settings → Automatic tracking and allow location "Always" so days are logged while the app is closed. It uses low-power monitoring, not continuous GPS. Without "Always", days are logged only when you open the app — manual trips and photo rebuild still work.

Restoring purchases

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Moving to a new iPhone

StayProof has no account. Use Settings → Export backup, save the file, and import it on the new phone (Settings → Import backup).
